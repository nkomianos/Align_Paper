## Corrections that change the handover

1. **Stage 1 is invalid for controller inference.** Comprehension 39.0625% and reversal 25% fail prerequisites of 90% and 80%. Training acquisition does not repair this. The operational STOP remains correct, but the handover's valid-negative label contradicts its own classification definition.
2. **Recency's original causal assay is invalid; its repaired endpoint is developmental.** The original intervention/control mismatch was substantive. The corrected switch effects are −0.0718106 and +0.7040548 percentage points, with no useful steering. The original runner discarded raw endpoint probabilities, and the local archive lacks its bound runtime preflight. The aggregate-verifier PASS is insufficient to claim full raw reproduction.
3. **Recipe's narrow failure survives with a reproducibility limitation.** Source A/B agreement and held-out C steering are different claims. Held-out steering is −0.095282/−0.2239422 points. Immutable model/preflight binding is repaired, and source selection precedes C, but per-case endpoint scores were not saved. Erasure ratios with near-zero denominators must not be advertised as meaningful effects.
4. **UNDO is a one-seed developmental comparison, and its uncertainty claim needs correction.** The protocol calls every split post-pilot DEVELOPMENT. Long-history counts are baseline 23/32, terminal SFT 24/32, canonical 28/32 and local rewrite 24/32. Local loses four paired histories to canonical and wins none: exact two-sided paired p=0.125 (one-sided 0.0625). A percentile bootstrap excluding zero does not establish a conventional 5% paired superiority conclusion. The local method has no observed advantage; parking is justified without calling the difference confirmed.
5. **C2C's parser repair cannot retroactively qualify the original assay.** The strict verifier returns BASELINE_INCONCLUSIVE_NO_UPDATE_TRAINING. Conservative reanalysis finds sender 113/128 versus fused 65/128; upstream parser fused 72/128. Four fused-only answers are complementary cases, not an achieved useful routing system. Sender-only inference is also faster in the recorded sequential comparison.
6. **Hybrid and feedback use repeated units in some bootstrap code.** Hybrid's 128 directional rows are 64 paired units. The corrected CI is [0.1471542358, 0.1902465820] logits around 0.16845703125, still far below its 0.50 gate. Feedback's pressure-pooled utility CI must cluster its 48 base cases, not treat 96 base×pressure pairs as independent. The zero-fabrication negative is unchanged.
7. **EP0 was ordered still images, not native video.** Native-video comparison occurred in the later 24-call interface diagnostic. Both fail to isolate the intended perceptual mechanism; the numeric-text diagnostic is itself only 4/6. The full 450-call gate and real-video thesis were never run.
8. **Several omitted runs strengthen parking decisions.** The two-pair coupling clarification has 20,480 completions over 40 new article clusters, with every cost-ratio CI crossing one. The full fitted-flow replay exposes and repairs a training-count confound without passing the corrected gate. Natural BERT drafts are all punctuation, invalidating the original lexical-correction assay. OPDLM strict parsing is 0/24 while permissive semantic correctness is 24/24, leaving a format failure plus ceiling rather than a useful correction task.

## Conclusions that survive

The exact provenance-authority, response-interface selection, recipe steering, semantic-ancestry remedy, effect-consistency UQ, visual patch-phase, feedback-gaming and visual-hindsight gates do not pass. These are scoped negatives about their frozen operationalizations. They do not establish broad absence of provenance effects, tool-interface problems, ancestry dependence, reward hacking or temporal reasoning problems. Recipe additionally carries the raw archival limitation above.

The strongest descriptive positives are ordinary apparatus competence, safe feedback recovery, a small recurrent-state binding signal, and complementary C2C cases. None licenses an ICLR method claim. Registered prerequisites outrank appealing endpoint arithmetic. A positive subcell among many tests, such as GPT-OSS inventory effect-vote gain, cannot rescue a failed conjunctive multi-domain gate.

No retired line merits GPU expansion on this evidence. A defensible single paper should concentrate its thesis and prospective confirmation elsewhere, rather than aggregate these heterogeneous failed screens into a general scientific law. The independent Hindsight/theory/literature audit must determine that paper's thesis and next experiment; this portfolio cannot independently certify it.

## Coverage limits

- This ledger is a claim/assay register, not a full re-verification of every filesystem root. The parent audit owns the broader inventory. Migration backups, prepared corpora, adapters, logs, retrieval snapshots, cells and retries are not independent experiments.
- Hindsight, PAHF/PUPPET and SDPO are assigned to another auditor and intentionally excluded, except the distinct visual-hindsight portfolio assay.
- Recency and Recipe original runners did not retain per-case endpoint probabilities/hidden states. Their committed verifiers can replay aggregate gate logic and hash manifests/adapters, not reconstruct raw endpoint metrics. Recency's remotely bound runtime-preflight record remains unavailable locally and revision main is not immutable.
- Feedback and effect-consistency original private key files are absent. This audit deterministically reconstructed BOTH public input and private key bytes from original builders; both key/public SHA256 pairs exactly match historical commitments, and full raw verifiers passed. They are closed reconstruction gaps, not missing-evidence exclusions.
- Stage1 numerical BF16 tolerance repair, Phantom historical-checkout requirement, coupling self-manifest exception and original failed command invocations are disclosed in receipts. A passing verifier means its implemented checks pass, not that the scientific hypothesis passes or verifier design is exhaustive.
- Original CPU coupling, semantic-ancestry initial run, BERT format repair and OPDLM native chat received raw/source inspection but not an independent dedicated full CLI replay here. DID archive and label arithmetic were replayed; its private-key controller interpretation was not independently reconstructed because label contamination already invalidates it.
- AgentUQ/calibration/prefix/matched-data roots, BeyondMasks contract audit, cluster-certificate audit, early byte/native-qualification constructions, generic preview/data fixtures and pre-bridge oracle snapshots were structurally inventoried, not fully scientifically reaudited by this portfolio subtask. No scientific endpoint is licensed from those unreviewed roots.
- Exact protocol commits not resolved are explicitly null in this ledger; cited protocols/manifests are evidence locations, not fabricated commit attestations. Frozen commits that were independently identified are recorded.
- No new model forward pass, optimizer step, GPU access, host access or locked-confirmation analysis was launched. Offline verifier computations and deterministic reconstruction used CPU only. Historic already-opened TEST data in completed effect/debt protocols are not current locked confirmation.
- The audit inspected local novelty claims/code. Independent current primary-source literature searching for the surviving thesis is assigned to the parent/literature auditor; this report does not certify every retired novelty assertion.
