# Memory Graft security paper claim ledger

This ledger is the authority for manuscript claims. A draft sentence must be
narrowed or removed if it exceeds the row below.

| ID | Claim | Evidence | Status and boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | Ordinary causal-LM fine-tuning can install the registered rare trigger mapping while the entire graft is frozen. | S1 frozen-table arms; G1; G2.2; G2.3. Independent training seeds, exact-match held-out contexts. | **Valid positive.** Pythia and Qwen base families, four sizes, two Pythia pairs and one Qwen-compatible pair, selected/fixed doses only. |
| C2 | A trainable deterministic table does not preferentially contain the ordinarily learned behavior under the S1 recipe. | S1 target-row vs benign/random-row deletion, five seeds per size. Upper 95% endpoints below 0.0011 versus 0.15 effect. | **Valid negative.** One Pythia pair and recipe; not a claim that tables cannot learn. |
| C3 | The S1 behavior is sufficient in the poisoned backbone without learned graft state. | S2b poisoned backbone + clean graft, mean ASR 0.9965/0.9949; reciprocal hybrid zero. | **Valid positive localization.** Component co-adaptation limits the reciprocal sufficiency interpretation; the poisoned-backbone sufficiency result is direct. |
| C4 | Target-row deletion is a functional intervention when behavior is known to occupy those rows. | S2e Pythia and G2.2 Qwen surgical writes: 98.30--100% removal; benign/random controls inert. A labeled post-hoc same-checkpoint audit finds S2e clean-NLL changes of -1.00e-5 and +1.21e-5 nats. | **Valid positive control.** Deliberate row optimization; overlaps User-as-Engram prior art and is not the novelty claim. The clean-quality result is descriptive and supports no preregistered threshold. |
| C5 | Gate closure does not explain the S1 null. | S2c trigger-vs-benign and poison-vs-clean gate contrasts, five seed means per size, intervals spanning zero. | **Valid negative evidence.** One injection layer in this implementation; absence of evidence for systematic closure, not proof of no gate role. |
| C6 | Qwen frozen-graft routing is reliable at N=64 in both tested sizes. | G2.3 five new seeds each; lower confidence endpoints 0.9969 and 0.9985. | **Valid positive.** Does not revise the N=16 result. |
| C7 | Qwen frozen-graft routing is reliable at N=16 in both sizes. | G2.2: 1.5B passes; 0.5B seeds 0.843, 0, 0.998 and CI crosses threshold. | **False; prohibited.** Correct conclusion is 1.5B positive and cross-scale negative at N=16. |
| C8 | Trigger specificity is uniformly exact. | Most runs zero, but one G2.3 0.5B seed has 140/1,024 near-trigger hits. | **False; prohibited.** Report seed-dependent specificity failure. Untriggered rate is zero in G2.3. |
| C9 | Results are bitwise reproducible at all scales. | S1 and many smaller-model rows are exact; G1/G2.2/G2.3 show 1.4/1.5B BF16 disagreement. | **False; prohibited.** Registered decisions reproduce; disclose raw disagreement. |
| C10 | Conditional memory is generally insecure or unable to store new information. | No such experiment. Surgical writes succeed; other memory architectures untested. | **Unsupported; prohibited.** Claim only that deterministic addressing does not force ordinary optimization to use the store. |
| C11 | The tested trigger mappings represent harmful deployment behavior. | Synthetic rare strings and one-token continuations. | **Unsupported; prohibited.** They are controlled storage probes. |
| C12 | The learned mapping depends on all-MLP updates and on the early six-layer band. | S3 checkpoint interventions, five seeds at each Pythia size; lower 95% necessity endpoints 0.986/0.991 for all MLP and 0.846/0.916 for layers 0--5; full replay exact. | **Valid positive.** The two overlapping groups were tested separately. S3 does not identify their intersection, and no coarse group passed sufficiency. |
| C13 | Early-layer MLP updates are themselves a necessary locus. | S4 prospectively selected early MLP layers 0--5. Necessity is 0.710 [0.506, 0.915] at 410M and 0.483 [0.135, 0.831] at 1.4B; sufficiency is zero; full replay is exact. | **Valid positive at 410M only.** The 1.4B result misses the fixed 0.15 lower-bound criterion and must remain a non-pass. The group is not sufficient at either size. |
| C14 | Increasing the table optimizer allocation can move the learned behavior into table-dependent state. | G3 at Pythia-410M: whole-table necessity 0.878 [0.542, 1.215], sufficiency 0.636 [0.168, 1.104], outside-table sufficiency approximately zero; exact full replay. | **Valid positive at 410M only.** Nominal trigger-row necessity and sufficiency fail. The 1.4B developmental selection changes under replay, so no cross-scale G3 claim is licensed. |
| C15 | The deterministic rows queried across the full trigger history can carry a sufficient table copy under aggressive table optimization. | G4 at Pythia-410M: 72-row all-internal sufficiency 0.648 [0.166, 1.130]; five prediction-exact replays. | **Valid positive at 410M only, with a narrow margin.** Specific necessity fails [0.092 lower endpoint]; final-row and incremental earlier-row claims fail. A benign marker shares 24 prefix rows and removes 0.958 ASR in one seed, so the result does not license isolated item deletion or tenant locality. |
| C16 | Aggressive table optimization makes learned table state causally necessary at a second Pythia scale. | G3.1 at Pythia-1.4B: whole-table necessity 0.9975 [0.9949, 1.0000], outside-table sufficiency 0.0025 [0.0000, 0.0051]; five new seeds and prediction-exact full replay. | **Valid positive at 1.4B.** Whole-table sufficiency formally fails because one seed transfers almost nothing, exposing table--backbone co-adaptation. Final-row sufficiency and row-transplant deletion also fail. The licensed cross-scale claim is table dependence, not a self-contained table module or item boundary. |
| C17 | A second injection depth and the public reference implementation's 5x table optimizer do not force the tested mapping into the graft. | G5 at Pythia-410M, layers 1 and 15: outside-graft sufficiency 0.9953 [0.9865, 1.0000] with grafts frozen and 0.9982 [0.9956, 1.0000] with split 5x table Adam; all tables update; metric- and prediction-exact full replay. | **Valid positive robustness result.** One retrospectively grafted backbone, one trigger/payload, and one moderate multiplier. It closes the single-early-injection and inactive-table versions of the confound, not jointly pretrained conditional memory or arbitrary optimizer policies. |
| C18 | Table learning rate causally moves the storage component through a sharp, scale-dependent transition, without reliably fixing the nominal final-row boundary. | G6 four-rate paired sweep, 16 new seeds per size; separately declared pooled fixed endpoint n=21 per size; paired quality deltas; G6-only and pooled temporal panels; full metric- and prediction-exact replay. | **Valid confirmatory result.** Primary new-seed whole-table dependence at 1e-1 is 16/16 at 410M and 15/16 at 1.4B; final-row sufficiency is 7/16 at both. In the primary temporal arm, 72-row history specific necessity is 15/16 and sufficiency is 11/16. The secondary pools give 21/21 and 20/21 dependence. Pooled 410M whole-table sufficiency and history sufficiency are both borderline 15/21 calls with Wilson lower 0.500436, and those pools mix 10M-token parent with 5M-token G6 clean adaptation. Row allocation varies and the shared-prefix control has meaningful effects in 4/16 new seeds. Clean NLL rises 0.0773 and 0.06894 (8.04% and 7.14% perplexity). The claim is limited to the tested graft, mapping, schedule, rates, and models. |
| C19 | The retrofitted graft is functionally used for held-out clean prediction after clean adaptation. | Frozen post-hoc paired intact/bypassed audit of all twelve preserved S1 clean checkpoints; exact repeat execution. | **Valid descriptive result.** Bypass raises mean NLL 0.05417 at 410M (6/6 seeds, bootstrap [0.02598, 0.08135], perplexity ratio 1.0557) and 0.001395 at 1.4B (5/6, [0.000344, 0.002732], ratio 1.0014); bypass improves NLL by 0.000218 in the remaining 1.4B seed. Clean-language contribution is strong at 410M and weak at 1.4B. No Qwen bypass audit was run. This does not estimate benefit versus a separately trained ungrafted model or jointly pretrained conditional memory. |

## Non-evidence preserved for audit

G2 stopped before scientific marker-dependent training because `" cobalt"` was
two Qwen tokens. G2.1 stopped before its first surgical optimizer step because a
local integer shadowed the training function. Neither supports a positive or
negative scientific conclusion. G2.2's runner completed every raw cell before
its aggregation helper rejected the preregistered three-seed interval; the
sealed rows were assembled without modification and fully replayed. G1's frozen
verifier failure at Pythia-1.4B remains a verification qualification even though
the primary trigger decision survived a disclosed diagnostic replay.
