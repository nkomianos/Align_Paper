# Focused persistence-data recheck

The repository already contains a completed public-response secondary audit
(PUBLIC_RESPONSE_AUDIT_RESULTS_20260905.md). Do not describe that route as never
run or simply unavailable. Its full-range attrition bounds do not identify the
sign of change in measured arm contrasts. This is not evidence that persistence
is absent; latent-state calibration and neural method benefit remain unproved.
No raw human records were reopened or sent to a model in this recheck.

One additional primary candidate, [Brief chatbot interactions produce lasting
changes in human moral values](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.21430), reports53participants,
within-person intervention/control scenarios, repeated judgments immediately
and14–16days later. Its data-availability statement offers data through author
request, not a public download. No author contact is authorized or performed.
The HTML route failed404; the primary PDF was inspected for design, follow-up
and availability. No participant-level reanalysis or replication occurred.

Repeated self-reported judgments would supply a later measured response, not
automatically a validated latent preference or a nonreactive anchor. A stronger
effect at follow-up is also not by itself evidence about next-turn self-training.
Our interpretation: this source does not presently close the Hindsight bridge.
No persuasion generation, targeting or intervention was implemented.

Disposition: retain as related empirical work, not a ready external validation
dataset or reason to restart parked training. Distinguish the completed older
public-response audit from this new source-availability screen.
