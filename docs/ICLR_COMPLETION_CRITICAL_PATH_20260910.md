# ICLR 2027 completion decision and calendar

**Current recommendation remains NO-GO.** This is a conditional planning
calendar, not a promise of sufficient results or authorization to extend the
current research window beyond September10,16:55UTC.

Official [call](https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2027/CallForPapers) rechecked
September10: abstract September18,23:59AoE; full paper September25,23:59AoE.
Python timezone conversion gives September19,04:59PDT and September26,04:59PDT.
Use earlier internal deadlines rather than relying on the final overnight hours.

The [author guidelines](https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2027/AuthorGuidelines) require
a genuine abstract and freeze additions to authors at the abstract deadline.
Main text is at most nine pages. New OpenReview profiles without institutional
email may require two weeks of moderation. If every author lacks an eligible
reciprocal-reviewing publication, each author may appear on only one such
submission. Actual author eligibility/profile status has not been inspected;
do not infer eligibility from our tools or add a nominal coauthor to evade a rule.

The [AI policy](https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2027/AIPolicyForAuthors) requires
disclosure in the manuscript and submission form. Our disclosure must cover
AI-assisted hypotheses, design, code, synthetic data, proofs and interpretation
as applicable. Do not claim human review has occurred when only automated checks
have run. Human authors retain responsibility for final claims.
