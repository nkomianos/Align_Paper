# Original OSH and CLARA: independent evidence audit

Research decision: neither existing manuscript/proposal establishes an ICLR-ready contribution. OSH contains an engineering recovery signal, substantial capability damage in V9, and invalid or confounded safety assays. CLARA offers a reusable neurosymbolic direction but its advertised unification and tractability guarantees do not survive mathematical checks. Do not expand either original claim with GPU experiments.

Sources were read locally; no GPU was contacted. Original OSH and proposal files were not changed. This is a scoped audit of the retained local evidence, not authentication of historical remote execution or replay of neural checkpoints. Reproduce numerical checks with `scripts/audit_osh_clara_cpu.py --osh C:/Users/nkomi/Documents/GitHub/osh --output artifacts/osh_clara_audit_20260905/CPU_AUDIT.json`. The receipt contains source hashes and explicit limitations.

The strongest surviving OSH statement is narrow: additive low-rank corruption can degrade this model, its inverse largely restores the saved capability subset, and separating later safety training from the antidote coincides with less capability loss than V9. The last comparison changes training designs and is not a causal identification of why V10 is better. Neither ordinary inverse recovery nor a lexical safety-score change is a paper by itself.

Minimal OSH salvage gate, if revisited: evaluate existing V10/V11 adapters on balanced semantic question pairs with positive/negative phrasing, option rotations, correct contextual tokens, free responses, benign requests and capability checks. Freeze these before viewing scores. Include base, restored-only and standard SFT controls. A general Yes-to-No logit shift must not explain the result. Cap screening at 1 H200 hour; missing actual adapter bytes or qualification failure stops it. Do not spend the budget on another poison scale sweep or 70B toggle.

## CLARA version-specific audit

Read the complete core text in `Documents/darpa_clara/Enloq_CLARA_TA1_Proposal.docx`, `Downloads/clara_enloq_submission/VOLUME_1_TECHNICAL___MANAGEMENT_final.docx`, and all 16 pages of v4 whitepaper text. Visually checked the v4 attention and treewidth pages. Local source extraction hashes are in `artifacts/osh_clara_audit_20260905/sources.json`; extracted private documents stay local and are not included in publication artifacts.

1. **Final proposal section 3.4 and v4 p14: false treewidth guarantee.** A square grid is planar, maximum degree four, and has treewidth growing with grid side length. A symmetrized four-nearest-neighbor construction on grid coordinates contains the grid as a subgraph. Fixed nearest-neighbor count therefore does not imply constant treewidth. Also out-degree is not undirected maximum degree. Grounded program width requires a separate argument. Replace with a verified tree decomposition / restricted graph class, and account for the semantic relations lost by restriction.
2. **v4 p4: false attention identity.** For A=[1,0], V=[0,10], low-temperature attention tends to 0, while the claimed max-plus product is 10. CPU evaluation at inverse temperature 100 confirms this. Ties also need separate treatment. Replacing GeLU alone does not tropicalize softmax, attention multiplication, or normalization.
3. **Final proposal section 3.1 and v4 p3: incorrect GeLU bound.** GeLU''(0)=sqrt(2/pi)=0.79788456, exceeding the stated maximum 0.40. For 16 uniform segments on [-5,5], the midpoint interpolation error on [0,0.625] is 0.0347952, exceeding the advertised 5/256=0.01953125 bound. Curvature-adaptive MSE claims were not independently reproduced and cannot substitute for uniform end-to-end error bounds.
4. **Useful correction already present in v4:** negative-log is correctly scoped to the Viterbi max-times semiring. It does not preserve ordinary probability addition. Best derivation and marginal probability can rank answers oppositely (disjoint 0.3+0.3 versus 0.5). The later v4 claim of equivalence to maximum-likelihood sequence scoring is overbroad.
5. **Established mathematics, not a new bridge theorem:** ReLU/tropical rational representation, provenance and differentiable logic are useful, but composing existing representations does not prove efficient compilation of a transformer or a new learning advantage. The short draft even writes a maximum of affine functions as the general ReLU representation; this is convex-only without a difference of convex terms. V4 improves that definition.
6. **Verification boundary:** a derivation certificate validates inference conditional on input facts. It cannot establish that a camera detection is true. AUROC is neither proof soundness nor a bound on false alerts. Correlated detections, repeated evidence, rule changes and temporal dataset splits require explicit evaluation.
7. **Complexity boundary:** a cheap gradient pass does not prove polynomial optimization to a good solution; bounded proof depth is not automatically a small proof tree. Fixed-k top-proof approximations, exact marginal inference and min-cost derivations have different semantics. They cannot be substituted silently.
8. **Bibliography needs reconstruction:** final proposal titles/authors for AMC, gradient AMC and Lobster differ from the cited works/v4 bibliography. Do not copy this bibliography into a paper.

Disposition: **pivot CLARA; kill the universal tropical-unification/constant-width thesis.** Keep frozen neural perception, explicit logic, provenance and rule editing. A narrow reliability experiment may fit our compute budget; the original two-year full platform does not.

Primary foundations: [ReLU/tropical representation](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v80/zhang18i.html), [compositional conformal neurosymbolic inference](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.15912), [active learning with conformal neurosymbolic evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.15750). Generic “logic plus uncertainty” is already occupied. New candidate protocols and novelty risks are in `ALIGNMENT_RESEARCH_OPTIONS_20260906.md`.
