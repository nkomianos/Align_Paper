## What survives and what changes

The restricted formal ambiguity and correctly scoped identification assumptions survive. The finite-state correction findings remain developmental, including the stronger ordinary-anchor comparator. The public study supports operational analysis of recorded attitudes, with attrition and selection limitations; it does not validate latent preferences or measurement neutrality. No newly trained useful delayed-label learner has qualified, and no general released-SDPO failure has been established.

The strongest defensible written thesis is now an **explicit measurement-assumption case study with carefully bounded empirical limitations**. It is scientifically honest, but its elementary theory, standard estimator and invalid neural assay do not currently constitute a strong ICLR methods contribution. The close-prior collisions documented in the independent literature audit remain unresolved. A format/capability failure cannot be renamed a causal-identification result.

**Kill the current submission plan built around demonstrated Hindsight latent identifiability or residual method superiority. Park the current neural recipe. Retain the restricted theory and public-response audit as reusable research artifacts.** Do not present “kill this submission plan” as evidence that all Hindsight learning or delayed feedback is useless.

## Smallest useful further experiment, conditional rather than queued

A new learning-only calibration protocol could compare direct supervised acquisition against a fixed-teacher and the current-teacher objective from identical initial adapters, using a newly frozen split of the already-developmental learning population. It must not retune against the now-observed96-base DEV results, relax their gates, or unlock confirmation. Same labels, update positions, held-out-task unit, teacher competence and semantic/NLL endpoints would be required. Strong simple supervised performance would locate a recipe problem; failure would question task learnability/budget. This would be developmental apparatus work, not the missing novelty argument. Based on the measured70-update stage, a small calibration is plausibly minutes rather than the old2–4hour estimate, with a conservative subhour cap.

That experiment should proceed only after a distinct paper hypothesis and a credible external estimand make it consequential. It is **not queued here**: the present mandate requires avoiding experiments that cannot rescue a meaningful claim. Published multi-token SDPO, fair same-information baselines, independent seeds, second-family evidence and a single prospectively specified confirmation remain downstream of successful acquisition and novelty review.
