## Measured, not estimated

| UNDO smoke, depth 4 | 48 | 1.832 seconds | 31.9 ms |

These exclude environment setup, downloads, model loading, tokenization, file
writes, hashing and verification. They are not end-to-end job durations. Cached
short-text inference is much faster than the initial 5–10-minute allowance.

Endo: independent-anchor initial-choice probability 0.999988, truthful correction
probability 1.0, choice mass approximately 1.0. Wrong-action hindsight advantage
1.2500 nats; adding the anchor restores only **0.06724** initial-choice
probability on conflicting cases. This is below the full-assay 0.10 restoration
criterion **on this smoke subset**, not a full-gate failure or thesis rejection.
UNDO: all four arms correct at depth 4, no stale-answer selections. No long
histories tested. Both decisions are `SMOKE_ONLY`; neither is a training result.
