# Benign feedback direction: novelty reassessment

Decision: do not launch a broad free-response feedback campaign merely to repair
the earlier forced-letter assay. Such a repair could clarify that result, but
the current scientific proposal lacks a differentiated contribution.

The recorded Qwen32 screen found30.5-point misleading-feedback harm and7.75-point
mitigation, but failed its prospective all-condition option-order gate. This is
not a validated paper effect. Four parameterized mechanisms are not200 independent
tasks. The strongest unresolved question from that run is whether response
representation explains the effect; answering it alone would be a developmental
assay audit, not evidence of a novel alignment method.

Newly checked primary sources:

- SycoBench-600, ACL Findings2026:
  https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.1759/.
  Its official abstract explicitly measures correction selectivity under correct
  and incorrect suggestions, across seven assistants. It distinguishes600 MCQ
  instances from272 normalized stems. Generic selective feedback acceptance is
  already an evaluated construct. This assessment read the official abstract and
  metadata, not every raw log or the full experimental implementation.
- Stubborn or Sycophantic? GEPA-Evolved Prompts Under Pressure:
  https://ahmedtaha.io/publications/stubborn-or-sycophantic/.
  The authors' page reports frozen prompt comparisons and cross-model transfer,
  separating useful updates from wrong flips. Some prompts reduce both, showing
  why lower reversal rates alone reward answer inertia. The page identifies the
  manuscript as a COLM2026 workshop paper to appear; no stronger publication
  status or independent reproduction is claimed here. PDF/raw evidence not audited.
- User Feedback Provides a Unique Signal that LLMs Can not Detect:
  https://arxiv.org/html/2609.02859v1.
  Located full text as a relevant boundary on feedback-information claims. No
  claimed refutation or reproduction of its experiments follows from this search.

Consequences: any future benign feedback experiment must retain beneficial
corrections as well as wrong-feedback resistance, use an explicit reference
contract, separate natural task units from paraphrases, and control response
encoding and realized reasoning cost. These controls are necessary, not new
contributions. A differentiated causal question or useful intervention with a
credible comparison to this prior work must be stated before spending GPU time.
No current free-response run is implemented or launched. The original negative
and invalid-assay records remain unchanged.

Portfolio implication: current narrow Qwen temporal-extraction evidence has failed
the second-family feasibility gate; the feedback direction additionally faces
close prior art. Neither justifies drafting a new results-backed abstract now.
Continue investigating unresolved benign directions while keeping submission
status NO-GO. Do not resume security probes or relabel developmental checks as
paper evidence to fill the remaining allocation.
