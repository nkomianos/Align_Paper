# Independent persistence-measurement feasibility, 5 September 2026

**Decision: pivot the empirical claim to temporally separated measured responses; retain the restricted identification theorem as motivation. A public repeated-response analysis is technically obtainable. No inspected source validates a latent persistent-preference instrument, and that stronger empirical claim should be removed from the current ICLR manuscript.** This assessment does not authorize a treatment-effect analysis, predictive endpoint analysis, model inference, human contact, or new data collection. None was run here.

The best feasible route is the public release for **Deep Canvassing using AI reduces prejudice toward undocumented immigrants**, version 4. Its current processed package omits the follow-up dataset needed by its persistence runner, but a historical public follow-up export links uniquely to exactly the reported 750 members of the current 1,108-person analytic cohort. The survey definitions establish the repeated items. Actual exported timestamps indicate approximately 40–49 days of separation, materially longer than the manuscript's stated mean of 35 days. We must use the observed measurement schedule and disclose this unresolved source discrepancy. This is a usable *candidate for a prospectively specified secondary measurement audit*, not an independently validated causal measurement channel or evidence for our neural method.

## What was inspected and what was not

I inspected primary manuscripts, repositories, public APIs, code, survey definitions and local schemas. I downloaded only explicitly public source files. Local descriptive processing counted records, missingness, measurement definitions, dates and one-to-one joins. It did not calculate treatment effects, outcome means, correlations, predictive performance, or train any model. No participant text, identifier value, IP address, location or individual attitude response was printed or sent to a model service. The public human files remain in the ignored `artifacts/` tree and must be excluded from every evidence bundle and release.

## Why the Science 2024 source is not the default

The [AAAS notice dated 11 June 2026](https://www.eurekalert.org/news-releases/1131247) identifies inconsistent screening and surplus spliced records from a code-merging error. It says the authors supplied a corrected pipeline and reported substantively similar results, while Science was evaluating them. That is an expression of concern, not a finding that the effect is false. [PubMed records the formal notice](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42275523/), DOI `10.1126/science.aej2383`.

This audit checked the [Dryad versions API](https://datadryad.org/api/v2/datasets/doi%3A10.5061%2Fdryad.v6wwpzh4h/versions) and [Zenodo latest-version endpoint](https://zenodo.org/api/records/12751762/versions/latest). Dryad exposed one public published version entry, version number 4, published and last modified 29 August 2024. Zenodo's latest record was still `12751762`, updated 29 August 2024. I did not find an official later resolution or corrected public pipeline in this bounded search. The primary publisher article fetch returned 403, so no claim is made that every publisher attachment was inspectable. Do not reuse the original effect sizes as a clean external replication or infer that the publisher review has concluded.

This supports planned randomized assignment. A processed analytic cohort alone does not verify execution, allocation concealment or unbiased postrandomization retention. In particular, exclusions such as failing to start the chat occur after assignment and require explicit handling before making an intention-to-treat claim.

### Timing correction

For the 750 matched records, exported main-study start/end dates span 28 August–6 September 2024; follow-up start/end dates span 16–19 October 2024. The elapsed time from main-study end to follow-up start has median **45.54 days**, minimum **39.86** and maximum **49.05**. Using main-study start instead produces median 45.56 days. The v4 paper reports a 35-day mean and three-day standard deviation. Export clocks and timezones have not been independently calibrated, but ordinary timezone offsets do not reconcile this difference. This is an unresolved source metadata discrepancy, not evidence that the recorded responses are fabricated.

Use the actual observed recontact schedule, approximately six to seven weeks, in any proposed estimand. Do not rename it a fixed five-week horizon. A source reconstruction receipt must retain both dates and this correction; it must not overwrite the archived evidence to match the paper's prose.

## What this route can establish

With an explicit target population and defensible selection assumptions, random assignment can support a contrast in **recorded attitude responses** at a specified observed follow-up schedule. Delayed-minus-immediate contrasts can test whether the corresponding randomized-arm response contrast changes over time. Within the observed respondent cohort, one can descriptively evaluate whether a supervision rule based on immediate responses predicts the later recorded responses. These are different estimands and need different protocols.

It cannot, without extra assumptions, distinguish a lasting change in latent state from a lasting change in response style, remembered study expectations, survey sensitization or a stable reporting artifact. All participants received earlier measurements; the design has no randomized no-baseline/no-immediate-measurement arm to identify measurement reactivity. Repeated items and a neutral-topic comparator help define and compare the measured construct but do not identify a known full-rank emission matrix. The smartphone comparator is an active discussion about another subject; it is not a guaranteed reporting-only intervention or a no-influence control.

Equal retention percentages by arm do not establish missing-at-random response. Selection may depend on unobserved follow-up beliefs in both arms. At roughly one-third missing follow-up, population conclusions need explicit attrition assumptions and bounded-outcome sensitivity analyses. We must also distinguish the recruited, randomized, final eligible and follow-up responder populations. An analysis conditional on the final processed cohort cannot automatically recover an intention-to-treat effect for everyone assigned a condition.

The paper's regression of individual follow-up change on immediate change should not be imported as a literal causal fraction of the treatment effect that persists. Both differences share a baseline measurement, and measurement error and stable individual response patterns can induce association. A preregistered randomized-arm contrast at each time and its joint change is a clearer operational target; an individual-change regression is secondary and requires its own assumptions. No such contrast was estimated here.

No experiment here evaluates a newly trained assistant action that then changes a user's latent state. The theorem's action-dependent post-action matching objective, next-query EndoPAHF prediction, and this human response study remain distinct. Nor should a prediction audit be described as optimizing political persuasion or identifying which users are most persuadable: that is outside the proposed task.

## Smallest next step and deadline decision

Before substantive reanalysis, freeze a separate protocol naming the measured item/scale, observed horizon, cohort, exclusion policy, missing-item rule, primary estimand, handling of attrition, and all comparators. Bind it to the two file hashes above, the survey definitions, code hashes and record-join receipt. Start with a deterministic local reconstruction of numeric fields and exclusions that never sends participant content to a model. A later predictive comparison needs a separately frozen split and same-label baselines; immediate-label carry-forward and a simple calibration/shrinkage rule are mandatory. Do not choose analyses because the published paper reported a significant contrast.

A first reconstruction and measurement audit is realistically **one to two focused CPU workdays**, including resolving timing and selection scope. This is a planning estimate, not measured execution time. A modest statistical script should run in minutes once specified; scientific decisions and review dominate. No GPU access or participant recruitment is needed to establish this route's limits. The source paper already analyzes response persistence and dialogue-pattern associations, so merely reproducing those observations is not a new ICLR contribution.

**Retain** the public-data route for a carefully scoped operational secondary audit. **Pivot** all current empirical language from latent persistent preferences to later measured responses wherever that is the actual target. **Kill** any claim that this release supplies validated latent-state ground truth, solves measurement reactivity, or closes the EndoPAHF/theorem bridge. A latent-identification human study would need an independently calibrated measurement model or an appropriate randomized measurement design; none is ready in this repository.

By 8 September, require a frozen operational protocol and a reconciled reconstruction receipt. By 11–12 September, require a useful method result beyond strong simple baselines, an independently reviewed novelty argument and a complete manuscript. If these are absent, prefer a later venue. Accessible public data removes one logistical obstacle; it does not convert the currently unrun neural method or elementary theorem into a submission-ready paper.
